Applying the strategy on candle data using the helper.

Brief

The section describes how to apply the strategy on candle data.

Details

Unlike the indicators, the strategies always receives two collections (bid and ask) of ticks as a source and the Update function is called every time when the offer price is changed. But some strategies are intended to work on the candles and must be checked every time when another candle is closed. To simplify development of such strategies a helper.lua library exists.

In order to use this file for development Add the following line at the end of your strategy:
dofile(core.app_path() .. "\\strategies\\standard\\include\\helper.lua");

This includes the helper code into your strategy.

Now you must also change the structure of your strategy.

1) Do not implement Update and AsyncOperationFinished methods in your strategy. Implement ExtUpdate and ExtAsyncOperationFinihsed instead.

2) Use ExtSubscribe function of the helper to subscribe on the required bar history.
