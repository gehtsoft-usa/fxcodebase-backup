## Polyline crossing entry order

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Apr 16, 2012 1:41 pm

Strategy enters a market order at the intersection of the price and polyline ([viewtopic.php?f=17&t=15667&p=30128#p30128](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=15667&p=30128#p30128)).

To use the strategy is necessary:
1. Import "EntryPolyline" indicator,
2. Draw polyline,
3. Use the name of the indicator in the "EntryPolylineOrder" strategy.

Download:

 [EntryPolylineOrder.lua](files/30130/EntryPolylineOrder.lua)

The Strategy was revised and updated on November 22, 2018.
