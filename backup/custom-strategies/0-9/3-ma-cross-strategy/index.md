Long
Fast / Medium MA CrossUnder
Fast > Slow
Medium > Slow

Short
Fast / Medium MA CrossOver
Fast < Slow
Medium < Slow

Exit (Optional)
Exit Long
Price < Slow MA
Exit Short
Price > Slow MA
