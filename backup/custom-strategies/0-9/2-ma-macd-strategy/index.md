## 2 MA & MACD strategy

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue Mar 22, 2011 1:24 am

Strategy based on 2 MA and MACD indicators.

BUY conditions:
MA1 crosses above or touches the MA2 and MACD > 0

SELL conditions:
MA1 crosses down or touches the MA2 and MACD < 0
