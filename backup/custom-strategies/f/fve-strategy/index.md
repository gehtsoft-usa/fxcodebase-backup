## FVE Strategy

**Apprentice** · Sun Dec 09, 2012 6:10 pm

![FVE Strategy.png](images/48183/FVE%20Strategy.png)



You can choose one of three entry options.
And one of two exit options.

Entry
1. FVE /(B/S) Level
Open Long
FVE/ Buy Level CrossOver
Open Short
FVE/ Sell Level CrossUnder

2. FVE / Signal
Open Long
FVE/Signal CrossOver
Open Short
FVE/ Signal CrossUnder

3. FVE / Signal / Level
Open Long
FVE/Signal CrossOver
only if FVE > Buy Level

Open Short
FVE/ Signal CrossUnder
only if FVE < Sell Level

Exit

1. FVE /(B/S) Level
Close Long
FVE/ Buy Level CrossUnder
Close Short
FVE/ Sell Level CrossOver

2. FVE / Signal
Close Long
FVE/Signal CrossUnder
Close Short
FVE/ Signal CrossOver
