## The 3 Duck's Trading System

**Apprentice** · Mon May 09, 2011 10:40 am

![The 3 Duck's Trading System Strategy.png](images/10445/The%203%20Ducks%20Trading%20System%20Strategy.png)



Long
Price <MA on Slow Time Frame (1.Duck)
Price <MA on Mid Time Frame (2.Duck)
Price / MA Cross Over on Fast Time Frame (3.Duck)
Short
Price> MA on Slow Time Frame (1.Duck)
Price> MA on Mid Time Frame (2.Duck)
Price / MA Cross Under on Fast Time Frame (3.Duck)

Exit Rules
If the ducks do not confirm one another.

 [The 3 Duck's Trading System Strategy.lua](files/10445/The%203%20Ducks%20Trading%20System%20Strategy.lua)

 

![Set the period of signal to the chart period.PNG](images/10445/Set%20the%20period%20of%20signal%20to%20the%20chart%20period.PNG)

Set the period of the signal to the chart period parameters to NO
