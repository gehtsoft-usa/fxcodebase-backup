![BB CCI STRATEGY.png](images/49340/BB%20CCI%20STRATEGY.png)



Open Long
price> upper band
and cci cross over buy level
Open Short
price < lower band
and cci cross under sell level

Exit Long
price cross under lower band

Exit Short
 price cross over upper band
