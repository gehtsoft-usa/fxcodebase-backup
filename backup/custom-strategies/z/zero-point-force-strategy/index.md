## Zero Point Force Strategy

**Apprentice** · Tue Dec 18, 2012 2:18 pm

![Zero Point Force Strategy.png](images/48724/Zero%20Point%20Force%20Strategy.png)



**Entry**
Cross Signal

Open Long
Up / Down Cross Over
Open Short
Up / Down Under Cross

Fractal/Slope Signal
Open Long
Slope changes from Down to Up
Open Short
Slope changes from up to down

**Exit (Optional)**
Yes / No

Selection

Cross Signal
Up / Down CrossUnder
Close Long
Up / Down CrossOver
Close Short

Fractal Signal
Slope changes from Down to Up
Close Short
Slope changes from up to down
Close Long

Any
Cross or Fractal, will result in the closing of positions
