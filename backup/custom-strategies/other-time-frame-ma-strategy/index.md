## Other Time Frame MA Strategy

**Apprentice** · Sun Oct 07, 2012 5:18 am

![Other Time Frame MA Strategy.png](images/41560/Other%20Time%20Frame%20MA%20Strategy.png)



This strategy was originally developed for the S & P 500 Index.
S & P 500 Month End, Simple Moving Average is based on moving averages,
obtained on the basis of monthly closing value of the S & P 500 index.

Long
Price / MA(12) CrossOver
Short
Price /MA(12) CrossUnder
Moving average Time Frame is always equal or greater then Price Time Frame.
