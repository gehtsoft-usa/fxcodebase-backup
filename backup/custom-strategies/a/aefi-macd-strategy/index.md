## AEFI MACD Strategy

**Apprentice** · Mon Feb 11, 2013 8:23 am

![AEFI MACD Strategy.png](images/54454/AEFI%20MACD%20Strategy.png)



**Open Long**
MACD > 0
AEFI > Level ( At least once in the past N Period)
MACD/Signal Line Crossover

**Open Short**
MACD < 0
AEFI < -Level ( At least once in the past N Period)
MACD/Signal Line Crossunder

**Optional Exit**
**Close Long**
MACD/Signal Line CrossUnder
**Close Short**
MACD/Signal Line CrossOver
