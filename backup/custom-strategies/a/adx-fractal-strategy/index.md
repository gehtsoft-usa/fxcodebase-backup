## ADX FRACTAL STRATEGY

![ADX FRACTAL STRATEGY.png](images/98852/ADX%20FRACTAL%20STRATEGY.png)



Based on the request.
[viewtopic.php?f=27&t=61871](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=27&t=61871)

BUY:
1. ADX IS INCREASING IN PAST 2 BARS
2. DMI POSITIVE> DMI NEGATIVE
3. CCI > BUY LEVEL IN PAST 3 BARS
4. APPEARANCE OF NEW SUPPORT FRACTAL(DOWN FRACTAL)

SELL:
1. ADX IS INCREACING IN PAST 2 BARS
2. DMI NEGATIVE> DMI POSITIVE
3. CCI < SELL LEVEL IN PAST 3 BARS
4. APPEARANCE OF NEW RESISTANCE FRACTAL(UP FRACTAL)

Exit (Optinal)
EXIT BUY: CCI CROSS UNDER BUY LEVEL OR PRICE CROSS UNDER DOWN FRACTAL
EXIT SELL: CCI CROSS OVER SELL LEVEL OR PRICE CROSS OVER UP FRACAL
