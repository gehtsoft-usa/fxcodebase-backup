## Highly adaptable Laguerre RSI Strategy

**Apprentice** · Sun Oct 30, 2011 9:44 am

You can decide which action strategy will take if you have Central Line or Overbought / Oversold Level Crossover.

Actions can be.
Close Position, Open Short or Long Position, take no action.

Trades can be in any direction.
The strategy provides CrossOver and CrossUnder the Line signals.

By default strategy does not take any Action.
You need to configure it.

 [Highly adaptable Laguerre RSI Strategy.lua](files/17130/Highly%20adaptable%20Laguerre%20RSI%20Strategy.lua)

Laguerre RSI indicator should be installed.
You can find it here.
[viewtopic.php?f=17&t=331&hilit=Laguerre+Filter](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=331&hilit=Laguerre+Filter)

The Strategy was revised and updated on November 19, 2018.
