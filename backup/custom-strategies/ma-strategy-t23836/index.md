## MA Strategy

**Apprentice** · Wed Sep 26, 2012 12:00 pm

![MA Strategy.png](images/40980/MA%20Strategy.png)



This is a simple strategy MVA.
Trading signals are generated by a simple one Price / MA Cross.

Long - Price / Ma CrossOver.
Short - Price / Ma CrossUnder.

As a option you can use Slope or Position of second Confirmation MA as filter.

Slope
Long - Up Slope
Short-Down Slope

Position
Long - Price > MA
Short - Price <MA

 [MA Strategy.lua](files/40980/MA%20Strategy.lua)

The Strategy was revised and updated on December 18, 2018.
