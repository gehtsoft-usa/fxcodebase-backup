![2.png](images/111120/2.png)



Open Long
TSI / Long Level CrossOver
and Price > T3
Open Short
TSI / Short Level CrossUnder
and Price < T3

Exit (Optional)
Exit Long
Price < T3
or TSI < Long Exit Level

Exit Short
Price > T3
or TSI > Short Exit Level
