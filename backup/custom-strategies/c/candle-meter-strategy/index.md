## Candle meter strategy

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Thu Mar 08, 2012 10:55 am

Strategy based on Candle Meter oscillator ([viewtopic.php?f=17&t=9706](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=9706)).

BUY condition:
Oscillator show [Number] following candles in the UP direction.
SELL condition:
Oscillator show [Number] following candles in the DN direction.

Download:

 [CandleMeter_Strategy.lua](files/27747/CandleMeter_Strategy.lua)

For this strategy must be installed Candle Meter oscillator ([viewtopic.php?f=17&t=9706](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=9706)).
