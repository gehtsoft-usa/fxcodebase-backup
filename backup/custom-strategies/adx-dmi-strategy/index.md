## ADX DMI Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Sep 27, 2012 4:37 am

![ADX DMI Strategy.png](images/41018/ADX%20DMI%20Strategy.png)



This strategy is based on Advanced_ADX2 indicator.
[viewtopic.php?f=17&t=8966](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=8966)
But do not use it. You can use standard ADX, DMI indicators.

Long
1. DI + /DI- CrossUnder and ADX > ADX Level
2. ADX/ ADX Level CrossOver and DI+ > DI-

Short
1. DI + /DI- CrossOver and ADX > ADX Level
2. ADX/ ADX Level CrossOver and DI+ < DI-

Exit (optional)
1. If ADX < ADX Level
2. DI+ / DI- Cross
3. 1 . or 2.
