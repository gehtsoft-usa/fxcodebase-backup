## Bollinger Aroon Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Dec 06, 2012 5:00 am

![BB Aroon Strategy.png](images/47975/BB%20Aroon%20Strategy.png)



Open Long:
aroon up > strong trend level
and aroon down < weak trend level
price cross over middle line of bb

Open Short:
aroon down > strong trend level
and aroon up < weak trend level
and price cross under middle line of bb

Exit(optinal)
Exit Long:
1.price cross under upper line of bb (optional)
2.price cross under lower line of bb(optional)
3.price cross under central line of bb(optional)
Exit Short:
1.price cross over lower line of bb (optional)
2.price cross over upper line of bb(optional)
3.price cross over central line of bb(optional)
