**Bollinger band is calculated based on RSI**

Open Long
RSI> Bollinger band Top Line
1. & 2. Time Frame
Open Short

RSI< Bollinger band Bottom Line
1. & 2. Time Frame

Exit Long
RSI< Bollinger band Top Line
1. or 2. Time Frame

Exit Short
RSI> Bollinger band Bottom Line
1. or 2. Time Frame
