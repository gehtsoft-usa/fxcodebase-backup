BUY:
1.FAST MA>SLOW MA AND
2.MACD> SIGNAL AND
3. MACD> BUY LEVEL AND
4. PRICE CROSS OVER FAST MA

SELL:
1. FAST MA< SLOW MA AND
2.MACD< SIGNAL AND
3. MACD< SELL LEVEL
4. PRICE CROSS UNDER FAST MA

EXIT BUY:
FAST MA CROSS UNDER SLOW MA

EXIT SELL:
FAST MA CROSS OVER SLOW MA
