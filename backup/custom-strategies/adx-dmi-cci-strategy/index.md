## ADX DMI CCI Strategy

**Apprentice** · Wed Dec 14, 2011 6:39 am

![ADX DMI CCI Strategy.png](images/20624/ADX%20DMI%20CCI%20Strategy.png)



buy when:
1.adx >entry level ,
2.dmi+>dmi-,
3.adx<dmi+,adx>dmi-,
4.cci cross over buy level

sell when:

1.adx>entry level ,
2. dmi->dmi+,
3.adx<dmi-,adx>dmi+,
4. cci cross under sell level

exit buy :

1. dmi+ cross under dmi -,
or
2. cci cross under sell level

exit sell:

1. dmi- cross under dmi +,
or
2. cci cross over buy level
