buy:

1. linear regression slope 1> buy level1 and
2. linear regression slope 2> buy level 2 and
3. price cross over middle line of t3 band

sell:
1. linear regression slope 1< sell level1 and
2. linear regression slope 2< sell level 2 and
3. price cross under middle line of t3 band

exit buy:

1.price cross-under upper line or
2. price cross under lower line

exit sell:

1.price cross over lower line or
2.price cross over upper line
