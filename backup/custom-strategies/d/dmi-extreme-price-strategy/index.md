## DMI Extreme Price Strategy

**Apprentice** · Wed Feb 29, 2012 4:29 am

![DMI Extreme Price strategy.png](images/27269/DMI%20Extreme%20Price%20strategy.png)



Buy
Price exceeds the high price of the bar when DI+ crosses above DI-.
Sell
Price falls below the low price of the bar when DI- crosses below DI+.

Threshold parameter is introduced.
Option enables the user to define additional Threshold zone
above / below, high / low of the crossover period.
