## Delete Duplicate Orders

**moomoofx** · Wed Nov 19, 2014 12:22 am

As requested here: [viewtopic.php?f=27&t=61097](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=27&t=61097)

 [DeleteDuplicateOrders.lua](files/97189/DeleteDuplicateOrders.lua)

A very quick strategy that removes duplicate orders if they have the same price. I.e. if there are 2 duplicates, it removes 1.

It is a very quick app to meet the requested requirements. Therefore, it doesn't care about the direction of the orders (if you have a buy and sell at the same price, it will still remove one) and it doesn't care about amounts or accounts etc.

Should people actually want it enhanced, then I'll do it.
