## DMI Strategy

**Apprentice** · Sun Jun 19, 2016 4:08 am

![EURUSD H1 (06-19-2016 1035).png](images/106814/EURUSD%20H1%20%2806-19-2016%201035%29.png)



Buy condition: DMI(+) crosses above DMI(-).
Sell condition: DMI(+) crosses under DMI(-).

Exit (Optional)
Exit Any position if ADX is lower than the default level.

ADX filter can be used (ADX>ADX_Level) for both directions.
