## RS dynamic line Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Jan 24, 2013 4:39 pm

![RS dynamic line Strategy.png](images/53517/RS%20dynamic%20line%20Strategy.png)



Open Long
Price cross over lower line( stream1)
Open Short
Price cross under upper line (stream2)

Exit (option)
Exit Long
Price cross over upper line( stream2)
Exit Short
Price cross under lower line( stream1)

 [RS dynamic line Strategy.lua](files/53517/RS%20dynamic%20line%20Strategy.lua)

Please Install RSdynamic_line Indicator.
You can find it here.
