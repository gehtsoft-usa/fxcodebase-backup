## VQ Bar CCI STRATEGY

**Apprentice** · Mon Dec 03, 2012 4:13 pm

![VQ Bar CCI STRATEGY.png](images/47781/VQ%20Bar%20CCI%20STRATEGY.png)



buy:
1.vqi -- green in color in past five bars
2. cci (period) >buy level and cci <= buy level in previous 2 bars

sell:
1. vqi -- red in color in past five bars
2. cci (period)< sell level and cci >= sell level in previous 2 bars

exit buy:
1.vqi changes from green to red or
2. cci cross under sell level

exit sell:
1. vqi changes from red to green or
2. cci cross over buy level
