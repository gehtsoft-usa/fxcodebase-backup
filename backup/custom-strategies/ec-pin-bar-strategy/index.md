## EC PIN BAR STRATEGY

**Apprentice** · Mon May 21, 2012 2:14 am

![EC PIN BAR STRATEGY.png](images/33621/EC%20PIN%20BAR%20STRATEGY.png)



buying conditions:
1. price > ema,
2. cci < buy level
3.bullish pin bar appearance { close = high,open> median}

selling conditions:
1. price < ema
2. cci > sell level
3. bearish pin bar appear appearance { close =low, open<median}

exit buy:
1. cci cross under sell level
2. price cross under ema

exit sell:
1. cci cross over buy level
2. price cross over ema.
