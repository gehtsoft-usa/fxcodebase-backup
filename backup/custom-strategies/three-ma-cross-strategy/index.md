## Three MA Cross Strategy

**Apprentice** · Fri Dec 07, 2012 9:39 am

![Three MA Cross Heatmap.png](images/48072/Three%20MA%20Cross%20Heatmap.png)



Open Long
If all three Crosses, gives long indication (green)
Open Short
If all three Crosses, gives short indication (red)

Exit (optinal)
Exit Long
If one of selected Crosses, gives short indication (red)
Exit Short
If one of selected Crosses, gives long indication (green)
