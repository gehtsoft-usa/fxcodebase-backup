## Bollinger in Trend Strategy

![CHN50 m1 (07-18-2022 1822).png](images/146794/CHN50%20m1%20%2807-18-2022%201822%29.png)



In bull market, we go to purchase if :

close > MA100

MA20 > MA200

% Bollinger < 0.2 and % Bollinger < 0.2 on previous candle (consolidation)

We close the trade if :

% Bollinger > 1
