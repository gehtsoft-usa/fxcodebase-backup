## BB ATR MACD STRATEGY

**Apprentice** · Fri Feb 08, 2013 8:06 am

![BB ATR MACD STRATEGY.png](images/54344/BB%20ATR%20MACD%20STRATEGY.png)



Open Long
MACD > BUY LEVEL
AND (BB TOP LINE- BB LOW LINE) < 1.5 * ATR
AND PRICE CROSS OVER TOP LINE

Open Short
 MACD < SELL LEVEL
 AND BB TOP LINE- BB LOW LINE < 1.5 *ATR
 AND PRICE CROSS UNDER LOW LINE

Optional Exit
Close Long
MACD < SL
Close Short
MACD > BL
