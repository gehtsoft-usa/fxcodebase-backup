## MA and VTDMI simultaneous crossover Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Apr 21, 2011 4:15 am

![MA and VTDMI simultaneous crossover Strategy.png](images/9894/MA%20and%20VTDMI%20simultaneous%20crossover%20Strategy.png)



This strategy gives Entry signals only if the two moving average crossover have same indication as Crossover of two components of VTDMI indicators.

It goes without saying, the signals are extremely rare.
