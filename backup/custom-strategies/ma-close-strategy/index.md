## MA Close Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Oct 24, 2013 9:34 am

[MA Close Strategy.lua](files/90317/MA%20Close%20Strategy.lua)

This strategy will close selected position,
if Closing Price cross over moving average.

Up Cross will Close Short Position.
Down Cross will Close Long Position.

Live and End of Turn, models are supported.

The Strategy was revised and updated on December 11, 2018.
