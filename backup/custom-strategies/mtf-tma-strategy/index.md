BUY;

EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN M15 > BUY LEVEL(M15) - In Pips
EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4 > BUY LEVEL(H4) - In Pips
EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4 (PERIOD)>EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4( PERIOD-1)
PRICE IN M 15 CROSS OVER LOWER BAND OF EXTREME TMA LINE

SELL:

EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN M15 < SELL LEVEL(M15) - In Pips
EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4 < SELL LEVEL(H4) - In Pips
EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4 (PERIOD)<EXTREME TMA SLOPE IN H4( PERIOD-1)
PRICE IN M15 CROSS UNDER UPPER BAND OF EXREME TMA LINE
