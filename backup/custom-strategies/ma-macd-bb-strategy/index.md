## MA MACD BB Strategy

**Apprentice** · Fri Apr 18, 2014 6:48 am

![MA MACD BB Strategy.png](images/93581/MA%20MACD%20BB%20Strategy.png)



INDICATORS
1.MACD
2.BOLLIGER BAND USES MACD OF CLOSE AS SOURCE
3.MA

Open Long
Close > Ma and MACD Crosses Over Upper BB Band
Open Short
Close < Ma and MACD Crosses Over Lower BB Band

Strategy is designed as End of turn Instance.
Trades will executed as the very end of the current period.

Exit function will not affect the position of other Strategys.
