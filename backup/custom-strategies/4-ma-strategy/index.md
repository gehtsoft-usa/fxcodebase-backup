## 4 MA Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Apr 05, 2012 11:52 am

![4 MA Strategy.png](images/29377/4%20MA%20Strategy.png)



The trend has been identified, based onas UP or DOWN based on the 120 and 240 period MA.

Up Trend - 120 Period MA > 240 Period MA
Down Trend - 120 Period MA < 240 Period MA

Enter long position only when trend is UP, and price close above 20 MA
Close position when price close below 10 MA

Enter short position only when trend is DOWN, and price close below 20 MA
Close position when price close above 10 MA
