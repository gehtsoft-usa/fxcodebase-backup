## Two Regression Lines MACD Strategy

![1.png](images/117002/1.png)



 

![2.png](images/117002/2.png)



Open Long
•	RLFast is Rising (Green)
•	RLFast > RLSlow
•	RLSlow is Rising (Green)
•	Price Close > RLSlow
•	MACD > Signal

Exit Long:
RLFast < RLSlow AND RLFast is Falling (Red)

Vice versa for Short
