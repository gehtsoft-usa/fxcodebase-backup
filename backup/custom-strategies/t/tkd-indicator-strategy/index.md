## TKD Indicator Strategy

![EURUSD H1 (04-13-2016 0923).png](images/105760/EURUSD%20H1%20%2804-13-2016%200923%29.png)



**"T/F Cross"**
Long
T / F CrossOver
Short
T / F CrossUnder

**"T/S Cross"**
Long
T / S CrossOver
Short
T / S CrossUnder

Exit (Optinal)
Exit Long
RSI < MA of RSI

Exit Short
RSI > MA of RSI
