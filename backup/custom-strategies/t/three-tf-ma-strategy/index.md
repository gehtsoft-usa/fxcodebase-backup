## Three TF MA Strategy

**Apprentice** · Wed Nov 02, 2011 3:36 am

![Three TF MA Strategy.png](images/17276/Three%20TF%20MA%20Strategy.png)



First, the use of this strategy is for now limited to holders of the beta version of trading platform.

Buy
Price/MA CrossOver
But only if we have confirmation on other two time frames
Price > MA CrossOver

Sell
Price/MA CrossUnder
But only if we have confirmation on other two time frames
Price < MA CrossOver
