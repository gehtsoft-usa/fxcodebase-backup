To work you need to have Generalized DEMA (GD)
[viewtopic.php?f=17&t=1302](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=1302)

This Strategy can trade this types of Signals.
Slope - A change in the trend of T3 MA.
Cross - Cross of Slected price and T3 moving average.
Both - Trade Both Slope and Cross Signal.

The Strategy was revised and updated on November 19, 2018.
