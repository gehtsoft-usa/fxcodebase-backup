## TDI MA STRATEGY

**Apprentice** · Tue Aug 28, 2012 12:08 pm

![TDI MA STRATEGY.png](images/39313/TDI%20MA%20STRATEGY.png)



INDICATORS USED:
1.TRADERS DYNAMIC INDEX(RSI PERIOD 50)
BUY ENTRY LEVEL:50
SELL ENTRY LEVEL:50
BUY EXIT LEVEL: 70
SELL EXIT LEVEL: 30
2.MOVING AVERAGE (PREFERABLY MVA) PERIOD 34 –PRICE APPLEID TO HIGH
3.MOVING AVERAGE( PREFERABLY MVA) PERIOD 34—PRICE APPLIED TO LOW

BUYING CONDITION:
IN TDI FOLLOWING CONDITION SHOULD BE SATISFIED
1.BUFF 1> BUFF5
2.BUFF5>BUFF4
3.BUFF1< BUFF2
4.BUFF1> BUY ENTRY LEVEL
5.BUFF1< BUY EXIT LEVEL

ALONG WITH ABOVE CONDITIONS PRICE CROSS OVER MVA 34 PRICE APPLIED TO HIGH

SELLING CONDITION:

IN TDI FOLLOWING CONDITION SHOULD BE SATISFIED
1.BUFF1< BUFF 5
2.BUFF5<BUFF4
3.BUFF1 >BUFF 3
4.BUFF1< SELL ENTRY LEVEL
5.BUFF1> SELL EXIT LEVEL

ALONG WITH ABOVE CONDITIONS PRICE UNDER MVA (PRICE APPLIED TO LOW-PERIOD 34)

EXIT BUY:
BUFF 1 CROSS UNDER BUFF 5

EXIT SELL:
BUFF 1 CROSS OVER BUFF5
