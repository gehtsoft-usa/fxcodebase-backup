## MTF ADX DMI STRATEGY

**Apprentice** · Tue Sep 18, 2012 2:44 am

![MTF ADX DMI STRATEGY.png](images/40487/MTF%20ADX%20DMI%20STRATEGY.png)



1. ADX AND DMI OF SMALL TIME FRAME
---- TIME FRAME : H1
-----ADX period :14
-----DMI period :14
-----trending level(S) : 25

2. ADX AND DMI OF MID TIME FRAME
---- TIME FRAME : H4
-----ADX period :14
-----DMI period :14
-----trending level(M) : 20

3. ADX AND DMI LARGE TIME FRAME
---- TIME FRAME : D1
-----ADX period :14
-----DMI period :14
-----trending level (L): 20

BUY:

1. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(L) AND DMI POSITIVE > DMI NEGATIVE IN D1 AND
2. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(M) AND DMI POSITIVE > DMI NEGATIVE IN H4 AND
3. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(S) AND DMI POSITIVE CROSS OVER DMI NEGATIVE IN H1

SELL:

1. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(L) AND DMI POSITIVE <DMI NEGATIVE IN D1 AND
2. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(M) AND DMI POSITIVE < DMI NEGATIVE IN H4 AND
3. ADX > TRENDING LEVEL(S) AND DMI POSITIVE CROSS UNDER DMI NEGATIVE IN H1
