## Moving average High/Low strategy

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Jan 18, 2012 4:20 am

Strategy use 2 moving averages: for High and Low prices.

BUY condition: price above High MA.
SELL condition: price below Low MA.

Download strategy:

 [MA_HL_Strategy.lua](files/23769/MA_HL_Strategy.lua)

For this strategy must be installed Averages indicator ([viewtopic.php?f=17&t=2430](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=2430)).
