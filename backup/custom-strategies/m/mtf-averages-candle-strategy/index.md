## MTF Averages candle Strategy

![MTF Averages candle Strategy.png](images/28285/MTF%20Averages%20candle%20Strategy.png)



Allows you to use Averages candle on two time frames.

First gives signals on trend change.
Second is used as a confirmation.

Optional Exit closes position when signal time frame change direction.

 [MTF Averages candle Strategy.lua](files/28285/MTF%20Averages%20candle%20Strategy.lua)
