## MA Strategy

**Apprentice** · Fri Dec 30, 2011 3:52 am

![MA Strategy.png](images/22028/MA%20Strategy.png)



This strategy is similar to the stardard Ma Advisor.
But unlike it, it allows independent definition of Entry and Exit signals.
Signaly are generated by Ma Crossover.

Entry
Long
Ma CrossOver
short
Ma CrossUnder

Exit Long
Ma CrosUnder
Exit Short
MA CrossOver
