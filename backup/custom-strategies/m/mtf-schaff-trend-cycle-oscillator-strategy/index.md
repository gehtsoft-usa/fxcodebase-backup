## MTF Schaff Trend Cycle Oscillator Strategy

**Apprentice** · Thu Nov 22, 2012 5:52 am

![MTF Schaff Trend Cycle Oscillator Strategy.png](images/45383/MTF%20Schaff%20Trend%20Cycle%20Oscillator%20Strategy.png)



In this example, the trading strategy is only Single (chart) time frame.
Optinal Exits / Filters are not used.

BUY:
1.SCHAFF TREND CYCLE > BUY LEVE ON H1 AND
2.SCHAFF TREND CYCLE > BUY LEVEL ON D1 AND (Optinal)
3.SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS OVER BUY LEVEL ON M15 (Optinal)

SELL:
1.SCHAFF TREND CYCLE < SELL LEVEL ON H1 AND
2. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE <SELL LEVEL ON D1 AND (Optinal)
3. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSSUNDER SELL LEVEL ON M15 (Optinal)

EXIT (Optinal)

EXIT BUY:

1. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS UNDER EXIT BUY ON M15 OR (Optinal)
2. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS UNDER EXIT BUY ON IN H1 OR (Optinal)
3. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS UNDER EXIT BUY ON IN D1 OR (Optinal)

EXIT SELL:

1. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS OVER EXIT SELL ON M15 OR (Optinal)
2. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS OVER EXIT SELL ON IN H1 OR (Optinal)
3. SCHAFF TREND CYCLE CROSS OVER EXIT SELL ON IN D1 OR (Optinal)
