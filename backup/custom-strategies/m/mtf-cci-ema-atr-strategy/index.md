## MTF CCI EMA ATR Strategy

**Apprentice** · Wed May 16, 2012 4:26 am

![MTF CCI EMA ATR Strategy.png](images/33087/MTF%20CCI%20EMA%20ATR%20Strategy.png)



Buy Condition:
1. close > open on H1
2. close > open on H4
3. close > ema on H1 + atr on H1
4. close > ema on on H 4+ atr on H4
5. cci on m5 cross over buy level

Close buy:
1. cci cross under sell level of m5
2. close < ema+atr on H1
3. close < ema + atr on H4

Sell is the opposite.
