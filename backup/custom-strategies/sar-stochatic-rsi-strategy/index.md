## SAR Stochatic RSI Strategy

**Apprentice** · Fri Oct 12, 2012 4:56 am

![SAR Stochatic RSI Strategy.png](images/41922/SAR%20Stochatic%20RSI%20Strategy.png)



Buy:
1. SAR signals buy
2. stochatic k and d are < stochatic buy level(40)
3.rsi >rsi buy level(55)
4. stochatic k cross over stochatic d

Sell:
1. SAR signals sell
2. stochatic k and d are > stochatic sell level(60)
3. rsi< rsi sell level(45)
4. stochatic k cross under stochatic d

Exit
If any of selekted Exit option has a contraindication.
