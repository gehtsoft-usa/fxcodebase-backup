## AMACD

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Jun 20, 2012 3:57 pm

MACD based on AMA ([viewtopic.php?f=38&t=20223](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=38&t=20223)).

Formulas:
MACD=AMA(Short)-AMA(Long),
Signal=SMA(MACD),
Histogram=MACD-Signal.
