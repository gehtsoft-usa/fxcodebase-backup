## Historical Volatility Ratio oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue Nov 26, 2013 3:23 pm

Formulas:
HVR = StdDev1/StdDev2, where
StdDev1 - standard deviation with Period1,
StdDev2 - standard deviation with Period2.
