## RSI of MA indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Mar 02, 2016 1:54 pm

Formula:
MA_RSI=EMA(RSI) with [MA_RSI_Length] number of periods, where
RSI - RSI(MA) with [RSI_Length] number of periods,
MA - EMA(Price) with [MA_Length] number of periods.
