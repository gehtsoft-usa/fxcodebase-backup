## How to Use the Auto Andrews Pitchfork Indicator

### **Uptrend Setup (Buy Signal)**
 **Example: AUD/NZD (4-Hour Chart)**
- The indicator detects **three pivot points** *(Low–High–Low)* to form the **pitchfork**.
- A **breakout above** the median band signals a **buy opportunity**.

### **Downtrend Setup (Sell Signal)**
 **Example: EUR/AUD (30-Minute Chart)**
- The pitchfork is drawn based on **three pivots** *(High–Low–High)*.
- When price touches the **upper band**, a **sell signal** is generated, with the **upper band acting as resistance**.
