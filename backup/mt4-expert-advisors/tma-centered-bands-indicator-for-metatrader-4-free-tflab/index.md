### **How the TMA Centered Bands Work**
The indicator consists of three bands that dynamically adjust based on market volatility:

 **Upper Band (Red):** Signals potential **overbought** conditions.
 **Lower Band (Blue):** Signals potential **oversold** conditions.
 **Middle Band:** Changes color based on trend direction.

 **Trading Signals:**
✔ **Buy Signal (Bullish):** Price exits the lower band, then re-enters.
✔ **Sell Signal (Bearish):** Price exits the upper band, then re-enters.

### **How to Trade with the TMA Centered Bands**

✔ Price breaks below the lower band and re-enters, turning the **middle band blue**.
✔ **Buy Signal:** Enter a long position when price exits and then re-enters the lower band.
