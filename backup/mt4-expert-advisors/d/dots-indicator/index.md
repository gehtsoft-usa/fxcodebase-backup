## Dots indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Sep 25, 2013 2:27 pm

Formulas:
UP - if MA(i)>MA(i-1) and MA(i-1)>MA(i-2),
DN - if MA(i)<MA(i-1) and MA(i-1)<MA(i-2).

The moving average is calculated for a Close price.
