Description:

At the end the best solution to the problem was to create a new indicator from scratch because the old one was not written correctly.

This indicator will let know the trader when 3 Moving Averages have crossed, but they need to do it under the allowed number of candles.

The indicator include both signals and alerts.

Except for news events with 100 pips candles, a 3 MA crossing will very rarely occur in 1 bar so that number should go from 2 and beyond.
