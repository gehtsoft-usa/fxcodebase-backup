## Trade Manager & Harmonics Expert Advisor

**edengrain** · Sun Mar 24, 2019 6:00 am

Hello fellow Traders

The EA is a trade manager and it's main purpose is to supply trading tools for traders. Sometimes it will look like your PC hangs but it is when the EA scans through the currency pairs and may take a couple of minutes.

PipGenius has 2 modes

Mode 1 = Auto_Trade
The EA will open 10 stop orders for each currency pair that comply to a set of rules. There is a " Close everything" button but it will take a while to close all the orders.

Mode 2 = Manual
The EA will give you tools to trade manually.
I personally trade with 99 and 1 and although I only have a few trades per day they are profitable and I can choose to increase the lot size.

TIP : To see which currency pairs have Gartley patterns make sure that Display_Harmonics_Indicator = true, Manual = true and that there are no open orders. In the three columns on top of the screen the Gartley patterns for that timeframe will be displayed.

Golden rules

1. The EA must be installed on only one chart.
2. Only one EA must be installed on only one MT4 terminal.
3. It cannot be shared with the same account number (if you bought the EA)
4. You cannot share the MT4 terminal with it and another EA.
