## Trendline EA

**cirque73** · Mon Oct 31, 2022 10:50 pm

Just draw a trendline or 2 with the diaganol icon ( / ). Then attach the Trendline EA. It worked live today with a small profit of $2. (It stopped out twice then took another trade)

As said before you can draw 2 lines and which ever is touch/closes first the EA buys/sells at that level.

What's also nice is that if the EA stops the trade out, if it goes the opposite way again it will execute another trade as said before.

I don't know if the trade is executed on touch or close of candle.
