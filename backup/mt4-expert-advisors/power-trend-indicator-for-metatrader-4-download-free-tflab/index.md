## How to Use the Power Trend Indicator

### Bullish Trend Signal (Uptrend Confirmation)
 **Example: AUD/USD (1-Hour Chart)**
- When the **PT histogram** crosses above the zero level, it turns **red**, signaling a potential **trend reversal**.
- If the trend remains weak, the histogram turns **blue**.
- As bullish momentum strengthens, the histogram turns **green**, confirming an **uptrend**.

### Bearish Trend Signal (Downtrend Confirmation)
 **Example: XAU/USD (4-Hour Chart)**
- When the **PT histogram** drops below zero, it turns **red**, indicating a **bearish reversal**.
- If the trend is weak, the histogram turns **blue**.
- Once the histogram turns **green**, it confirms a **strong downtrend**.

- **PT_chart** *(Default: 5)* – Aggregates histogram candles for trend smoothing.
- **PT_period** *(Default: 1)* – Defines the period for calculating trend strength.

### **Pro Tip:**
 For **short-term trading**, use **PT_period = 1-3** for faster signals.
 For **long-term trading**, set **PT_period = 5-10** to filter out noise.
