## Custom price

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Thu Jun 27, 2013 11:24 am

Formula:
Custom price = (Coeff_Open*Open+Coeff_High*High+Coeff_Low*Low+Coeff_Close*Close)/Sum_Coeff, where
Sum_Coeff = Coeff_Open+Coeff_High+Coeff_Low+Coeff_Close.
