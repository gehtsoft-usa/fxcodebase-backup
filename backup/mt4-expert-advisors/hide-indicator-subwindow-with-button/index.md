## Hide Indicator Subwindow with button

**pipibrasci** · Mon Sep 13, 2021 2:17 pm

A very good tool for your mt4. You can place upto 5 indicators in MT4 subwindows and shrink/show them by just creating equal number of subwindow buttons/ clicking on the buttons. You can create as many subwindows buttons as you wish and select what subwindow you want to see or hide.
