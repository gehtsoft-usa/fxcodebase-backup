1. Line/Filter

RSI < 30 - Oversold

2. Line/Filter
RSI > 50 - Buy Zone
RSI < 50 - Sell Zone

3. Line/Filter
RSI > MVA of RSI - Up Trend
RSI < MVA of RSI - Up Trend
