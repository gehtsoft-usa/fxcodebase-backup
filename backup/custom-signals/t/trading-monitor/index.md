## Trading Monitor

**Victor.Tereschenko** · Thu Feb 15, 2018 1:11 am

Shows a notification when a trade is opened and closed with some information about the trade (P/L, whether the trade was closed by stop or limit, open/close rate...)

The next notifications are supported:

- Text alert
- Sound alert
- Email
- Telegram message (beta)

 [trading_monitor.bin](files/117761/trading_monitor.bin)
