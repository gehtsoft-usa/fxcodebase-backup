## Awesome Oscillator Signal

**Apprentice** · Mon May 31, 2010 11:53 am

![Awesome Oscillator Signal.png](images/2320/Awesome%20Oscillator%20Signal.png)

*Awesome Oscillator Signal*



Signal is generated when Awesome Oscillator crosses Over/Under the zero line.

AO can be used in two additional cases.

Saucer Signal and Twin Peaks signals.

**Saucer Signal**
AO > 0
Buy -Signal is generated when the bar chart reversed its direction from the downward to upward.
AO < 0
Sell - Signal is generated when the bar chart reversed its direction from the upward to downward.

In this version I put Saucer Signal, I will soon add Twin Peaks signals.
