## ADX AROON Strategy

**Apprentice** · Sat Sep 11, 2010 8:48 am

![ADX AROON Strategy.png](images/4400/ADX%20AROON%20Strategy.png)



This signal is written on the basis of a request

 

![putting together1.jpg](images/4400/putting%20together1.jpg)



Signal is given when the ADX line crosses up through a horizontal line placed on the 25 mark.

 Signal Confirmation
 If Aroon indicator is anywhere between 95 and 100.
Aroon Down line for Sell signal and Aroon Up line for Buy .

The is one exception is if the signals were created by an exceptionally large bar.
 Then the signals are ignored.

The strategy has the option of giving signals when the ADX meet the criteria.
Not only on the ADX croossover.
