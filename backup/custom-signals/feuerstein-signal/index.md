## Feuerstein Signal

**Apprentice** · Sun Apr 25, 2010 6:48 am

This signal is made at the request of users, combines multiple indicators.
Heikin-Ashi, MVA 13 and 21, ADX, STOCHASTIK, RSI and CCI.
If 4 out of 5 indicators gives the same indication gives the signal.
ADX is always checked.
This is the first working version.
