## Moving Average Envelope Signal

**Apprentice** · Tue May 31, 2011 4:24 am

![Moving Average Envelope Signal.png](images/11210/Moving%20Average%20Envelope%20Signal.png)



This signal provides a simple signal.
When Price cross Lower or upper band.

The indicator provides an ability to:
- choose the moving average method (MVA, EMA, LWMA, SMMA and so on)
- specify the band width either in 1/100 of percent or in pips.

 [Moving Average Envelope Signal.lua](files/11210/Moving%20Average%20Envelope%20Signal.lua)
