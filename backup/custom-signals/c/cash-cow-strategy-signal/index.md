## Cash Cow Strategy Signal

**Apprentice** · Sun Jun 13, 2010 4:52 am

![CCS.png](images/2517/CCS.png)

*Cash Cow Strategy Signal*



This Signal was created based on requests,

**Entry Signal (Long)**
EMA (5) & EMA(20) are raising
EMA (5) > EMA (20)

Close > Lower 1.2% * Ema (5) Envelope
Close < Ema (5)

High < Upper 0.6% * Ema (5) Envelope
High > Ema (5)

**Entry Signal (Short)**
EMA (5) & EMA(20) are falling
EMA (5) < EMA (20)

Close < Higher 1.2% * Ema (5) Envelope
Close > Ema (5)

Low > Lower 0.6% * Ema (5) Envelope
Low < Ema (5)
