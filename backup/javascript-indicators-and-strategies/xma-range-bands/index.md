## XMA Range Bands

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue Aug 07, 2018 12:26 pm

Formulas:
XMA=MA(Price),
Upper=XMA+MA(Range),
Lower=XMA-MA(Range), where
Range=High-Low.
