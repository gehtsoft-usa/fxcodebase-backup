## Inverse reaction oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Mar 30, 2019 12:04 pm

Formulas:
CO[i] = Close[i]-Open[i],
U[i] = Coeff(MA(Abs(CO))),
D[i] = -U[i].
