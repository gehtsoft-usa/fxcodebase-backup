## Minimum-maximum moving average

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Fri Jun 02, 2017 2:17 pm

Formula:
MinMaxMA[i]=(Min+Max)/2, where
Min, Max - minimum and maximum prices in range from (i-Period+1) to (i).
