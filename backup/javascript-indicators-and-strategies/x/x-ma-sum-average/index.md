## X MA Sum Average

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sun Aug 19, 2018 2:11 pm

XMaSumAvgSum or XMA Averages calculated Average as the average of All moving averages from Start-End Range.

XMA = (MA[Start] + MA[Start+1] + .... + MA[End] )/ (End-Start+1)
