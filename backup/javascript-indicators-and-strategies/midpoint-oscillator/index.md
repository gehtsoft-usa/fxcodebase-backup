## Midpoint Oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Jan 06, 2018 5:06 pm

As described in the Nov 1991 edition of S&C magazine.

%M=100* (2*Close-High(N)-Low(N))/(High(N)-Low(N)).
