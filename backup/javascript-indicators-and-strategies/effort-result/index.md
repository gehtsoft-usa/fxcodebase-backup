## Effort Result

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Aug 15, 2018 2:28 pm

Formula:
ER = ROC/MaxVol, where
ROC[i] = 100*(Price[i]/Price[i-Length]-1),
MaxVol - maximum volume at range from (i-Length+1) to (i).
