## Bulls-Bears eyes oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 12:12 pm

Indicator values ​​are calculated as the sum of the indicators Bears Power and Bulls Power, averaged with an algorithm Laguerre.
