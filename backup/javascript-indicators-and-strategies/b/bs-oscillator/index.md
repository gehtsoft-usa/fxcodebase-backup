## BS oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Fri Jun 02, 2017 12:05 pm

Formula:
BS = StdDev(PA)/StdDev(NA), where
StdDev - standard deviation,
PA, NA - positive and negative candle body size (Close-Open).
