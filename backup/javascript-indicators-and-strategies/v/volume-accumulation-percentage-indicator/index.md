## Volume Accumulation Percentage Indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Oct 28, 2017 2:23 pm

Formula:
VA = Volume * ((Close-Low)-(High-Close))/(High-Low).
TVA= N period Sum of VA
TV = N period Sum of Volume
VACC[period] = (TVA / TV) * 100
