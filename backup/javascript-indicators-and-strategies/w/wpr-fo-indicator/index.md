## WPR_FO indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Jul 05, 2017 11:40 am

Formula:
WPR_FO=(Exp(2*MA)-1)/(Exp(2*MA)+1), where
MA is a moving average of [0.1*(RLW+50)],
RLW - Williams Percent Range.
