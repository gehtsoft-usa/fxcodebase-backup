## RSIOMA oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue May 01, 2018 1:43 pm

Formulas:
RSIOMA = 50 - 100/res, where
res = 1+p/n,
p, n - sum of positive and negative changes of Momentum.
Signal - moving average of RSIOMA.
