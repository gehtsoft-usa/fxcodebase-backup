## Point of Balance

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Oct 28, 2017 10:21 am

Formulas:
BL = (MinH+MaxH)/2,
BR = (MinL+MaxL)/2,
POB = (BL+BR)/2, where
MinH, MaxH - minimum and maximum HIGH prices at range from (i-Length) to i,
MinL, MaxL - minimum and maximum LOW prices at range from (i-Length) to i.
