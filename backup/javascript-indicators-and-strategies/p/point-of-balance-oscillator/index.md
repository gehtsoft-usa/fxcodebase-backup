## Point of balance oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Jan 06, 2018 5:18 pm

Formula:
BLF = ((HH[n]+LH[n]) /2)
BRF = ((HL[n]+LL[n]) /2)
POB = ((BLF-BRF) /2) + BRF.
