## Slingshot indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 3:24 pm

Basically this indicator draw the two momentum indicators Lines.
First as Bar, Second as Line.

Formulas:
Histogram[i] = Price1[i]-Price1[i-Length1],
Line[i] = Price2[i]-Price2[i-Length2].
