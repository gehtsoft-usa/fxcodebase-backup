## Vo oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed May 02, 2018 2:58 pm

Formulas:
Vo[i] = MA(Range), where
Range = Max-Min,
Max, Min - maximum and minimum prices at range from (i-Period+1) to i.
