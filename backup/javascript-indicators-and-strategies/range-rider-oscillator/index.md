## Range Rider oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed May 02, 2018 12:26 pm

Formula:
RR=100*MA1/MA2, where
MA1=MA(ATR) with [Period1] number of periods,
MA2=MA(ATR) with [Period2] number of periods.
