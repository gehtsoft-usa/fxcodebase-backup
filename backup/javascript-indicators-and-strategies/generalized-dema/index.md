## Generalized DEMA

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 12:37 pm

Formula:
GD = (1+VF)*EMA1-VF*EMA2, where
EMA2 - EMA(EMA1) with [Length] number of periods,
EMA1 - EMA(Price) with [Length] number of periods.
