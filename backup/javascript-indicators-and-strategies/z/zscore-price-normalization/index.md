## ZScore Price Normalization

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Mar 30, 2019 4:51 pm

ZScore = (Close - MVA of Close)/ Stdev of Close;

Z Scope basically translate the movement of Price within the Bollinger Bands to move within a fixed deviation channel.
