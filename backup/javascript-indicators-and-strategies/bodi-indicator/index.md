## BoDi indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Thu Apr 12, 2018 11:51 am

Indicator described in Forex Magazine issue #438.

Formula:
BoDi[i]=sqrt(S/Period), where
S - sum (Price[i-k]-MA[i]) for k from 0 to (Period-1).
