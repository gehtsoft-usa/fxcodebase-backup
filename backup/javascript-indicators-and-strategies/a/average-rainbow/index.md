## Average rainbow

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Mar 30, 2019 11:09 am

Formulas:
Average rainbow = (MA[1]+MA[2]+...+MA[Number])/Number, where
MA[1] = MA(Price),
MA[2] = MA(MA[1]),
.......................
MA[N] = MA(MA[N-1]).
