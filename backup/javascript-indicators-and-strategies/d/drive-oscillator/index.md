## Drive oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 12:25 pm

Up (Green) = ((High - Open) + (Close - Low))/2, averaged for the selected period;
Down (Red) = ((Open - Low) + (High - Close))/2, averaged for the selected period.
