## Pain oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed May 02, 2018 12:07 pm

Formula:
Pain = (3*MA(Close) - MA(Open) - MA(High) - MA(Low))/2, where
MA - moving average with [Length] number of periods and [Method] type.
