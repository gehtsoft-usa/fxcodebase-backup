## Gopalakrishnan Range Index

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue May 01, 2018 11:29 am

Formulas:
GAPO[i] = Log(Max-Min)/Log(Length), where
Log - logarithm,
Max, Min - maximum and minimum prices at the range from (i-Length+1) to (i).
