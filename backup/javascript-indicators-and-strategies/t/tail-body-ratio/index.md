## Tail / Body Ratio

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 5:37 pm

This indicator will give a signal in the two cases.
1) Candlestick wick / tail is greater than candle body.
(Body* Multiplier) < Tail
2) Candlestick wick / tail is greater than the rest of the candle.
( (Body+ Opposite Tail) * Multiplier) < Tail
