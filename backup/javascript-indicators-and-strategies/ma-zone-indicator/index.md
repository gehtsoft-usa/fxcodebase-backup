## MA Zone indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Sat Nov 24, 2018 3:06 pm

Formulas:
Central = MA,
Top1 = Central+Level1,
Bottom1 = Central-Level1,
Top2 = Central+Level2,
Bottom2 = Central-Level2,
Top3 = Central+Level3,
Bottom3 = Central-Level3, where
MA - Moving average(Price) with [Length] number of periods and [Method] type.
