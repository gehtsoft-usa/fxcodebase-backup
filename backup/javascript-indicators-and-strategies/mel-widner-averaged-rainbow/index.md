## Mel Widner Averaged Rainbow

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Aug 06, 2018 2:05 pm

Formula:
Average Rainbow = (5*MA[1] +4*MA[2]+3*MA[3] +2*MA[4] +MA[5] ... + MA[10]) /20.
