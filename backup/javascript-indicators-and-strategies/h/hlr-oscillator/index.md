## HLR oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Fri Jun 02, 2017 2:03 pm

Formula:
HLR[i]=100*(MidPrice-Min)/(Max-Min), where
MidPrice - median price,
Max and Min - maximum and minimum prices at range from (i-Period) to (i).
