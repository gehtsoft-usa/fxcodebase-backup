## Kurtosis

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed May 02, 2018 11:42 am

The Kurtosis is a market sentiment indicator.
The formula for the Kurtosis is as follows:

MVA(3) of EMA (66) of ( Momentum[period] - Momentum[period-1] ).
