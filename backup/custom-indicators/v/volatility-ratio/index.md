![Volatility Ratio.png](images/92615/Volatility%20Ratio.png)



A.K.A. Schwager Volatility Ratio by Jack D. Schwager
Volatility Ratio (VR) = Today’s True Range/True Range over N number of days

 [Volatility Ratio.lua](files/92615/Volatility%20Ratio.lua)

Try to identify trading range and potential trend reversal. Ranges between 0.01-1.00.
Predicts the reversal points if are greater then 0.5.
