## Slope in Degrees

**Apprentice** · Fri Jul 04, 2014 5:42 am

![Slope in Degrees.png](images/94756/Slope%20in%20Degrees.png)



Will give you the angle between the two points of any Source Stream.
By default period is set to 1
The angle is then calculated between the current and previous point of Source Stream.

 [Slope in Degrees.lua](files/94756/Slope%20in%20Degrees.lua)

 [Slope in Degrees with Alert.lua](files/94756/Slope%20in%20Degrees%20with%20Alert.lua)

Compatibility issue fixed.
