## Spread Normalized indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Jan 16, 2012 9:27 am

Formulas:
Spread Normalized=(MA(Spr)-Spr)/sDev, where
Spr=Price(First market)-Price(Second market),
sDev=Abs(Spr)/sqrt(Period).
