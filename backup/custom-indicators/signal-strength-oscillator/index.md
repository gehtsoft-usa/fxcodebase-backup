[viewtopic.php?f=18&t=63713](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=18&t=63713)

Supported filters are RSI, Stochastic, MA and CCI.
Indicator will sum up individual indications.

RSI > OB (-1)
RSI< OS (1)

CCI > OB (-1)
CCI < OS (1)

Price > MA (-1)
Price < MA (1)

Stochastic > OB (-1)
Stochastic < OS (1)
