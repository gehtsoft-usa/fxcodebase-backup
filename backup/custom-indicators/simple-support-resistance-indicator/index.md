## Simple Support/Resistance indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Wed Oct 09, 2013 6:02 pm

Formulas:
Support[i] = 2*Min-Max,
Resistance[i] = 2*Max-Min, where
Max, Min - maximum and minimum prices at range from (i-Length) to (i-1).
