## Relative Aggression Bars

**Apprentice** · Wed Apr 10, 2013 5:35 am

![Relative Aggression Bars.png](images/58551/Relative%20Aggression%20Bars.png)



The indicator will show periods with increased activity.

Activity is calculated as the ratio between current period change,
and normalization period maximum change.

You can define Moderat, Strong and Extreme Levels.

You can choose between three filters.
ATR, Volume and Closing Price.
