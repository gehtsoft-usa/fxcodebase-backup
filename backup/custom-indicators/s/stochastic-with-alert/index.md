## Stochastic with Alert

**Apprentice** · Wed Apr 24, 2013 6:01 am

![Stochastic with Alert.png](images/59878/Stochastic%20with%20Alert.png)



This indicator provides Audio / Email Alerts, for your Stochastic indicator,
It is possible to define a total of three separate signals.
K/D Line Cross
K / Overbought Line Cross
K / Oversold Line Cross

 [Stochastic with Alert.lua](files/59878/Stochastic%20with%20Alert.lua)

Dec 9, 2015: Compatibility issue Fix. _Alert helper is not longer needed.

The indicator was revised and updated
