## Simplified MTF MCP Bollinger bands View

**Apprentice** · Mon Nov 15, 2021 9:30 am

![SIMPLIFIED MTF MCP BB VIEW(UNK) (11-15-2021 1527).png](images/144284/SIMPLIFIED%20MTF%20MCP%20BB%20VIEW%28UNK%29%20%2811-15-2021%201527%29.png)



A rectangle that borders a numeric indication.
Price> 100 of BB Top Line - Green Color
Price <0 of BB Bottom Line - Red
Price> 0 and Price <100 - Gray

The numeric indication is the change in the Bollinger bands values from the last period.
