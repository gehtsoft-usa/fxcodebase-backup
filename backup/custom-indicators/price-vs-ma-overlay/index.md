## Price vs MA Overlay

**Apprentice** · Mon May 30, 2016 2:42 pm

![EURUSD m1 (05-30-2016 2110).png](images/106517/EURUSD%20m1%20%2805-30-2016%202110%29.png)



- Price is below one EMA/SMA, color of the candlesticks is red .
- Price closes above the EMA/SMA, color of the candlesticks is green.

Second option, two MA's.

- When price is above both EMA/SMA's, color of the candlesticks is green.
- When price is between the two EMA/SMA's, color of the candlesticks is gray.
- When price is below both EMA/SMA's, color of the candlesticks is red.
