 Shaded Moving Average
Description: This indicator plots a moving average line with shaded bands above and/or below, offset by a user-defined distance in pips. The shaded area visually resembles Bollinger Bands but is based on a static pip offset rather than standard deviations

Use Cases:
Trend Identification:
Quickly identify when the price is consistently above or below a moving average.
Support and Resistance:
Use shaded bands as visual cues for potential support and resistance levels.
Confluence Zones:
Combine shaded bands with other indicators for enhanced decision-making.
