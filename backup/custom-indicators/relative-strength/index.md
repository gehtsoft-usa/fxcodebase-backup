## Relative Strength

**Apprentice** · Tue Mar 19, 2013 3:24 pm

![Relative Strength.png](images/56916/Relative%20Strength.png)



Indicator will compare two instruments according to the formula.
RS =( (A2/B2) -(A1/B1))/(A1/B1)

A1 = Beginning price of chart Instrument
A2 = Ending price of chart Instrument
B1 = Beginning price of second Instrument
B2 = Ending price of second Instrument
