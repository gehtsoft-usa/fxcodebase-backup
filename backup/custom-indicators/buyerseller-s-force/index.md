## BuyerSeller’s FORCE

**Apprentice** · Thu Jun 30, 2022 8:07 am

![EURUSD m1 (06-30-2022 1505).png](images/146560/EURUSD%20m1%20%2806-30-2022%201505%29.png)



The FORCE of sellers can be measured by subtracting CLOSE from HIGH because they are pushing prices downwards.
The FORCE of buyers can be measured by subtracting CLOSE from LOW (or you can make it the other way round or use ABS()) as they push prices upwards.
