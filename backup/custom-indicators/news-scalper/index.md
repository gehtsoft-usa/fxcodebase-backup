## News Scalper

**Apprentice** · Mon Oct 20, 2025 5:55 pm

![EURUSD m1 (10-21-2025 0048).png](images/160926/EURUSD%20m1%20%2810-21-2025%200048%29.png)



Purpose
This indicator acts as a semi-automated trading assistant for news-based scalping — allowing traders to:
Predefine distances above/below price
Instantly arm and deploy an OCO straddle before volatile events
Quickly execute or close trades manually from the chart

Trading Tools (Context Menu)

When you right-click on the chart, you can:

Open LONG now → immediately place a market BUY order

Open SHORT now → immediately place a market SELL order

Arm now → set a reference level at the current price (center of straddle)

Place OCO Straddle → automatically place two stop-entry orders (BuyStop above, SellStop below)

Close all (CustomID) → close all trades tagged with your custom ID
