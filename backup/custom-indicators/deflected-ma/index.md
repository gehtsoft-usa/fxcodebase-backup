## Deflected MA

**Apprentice** · Fri Dec 23, 2011 7:27 am

![Deflected MA.png](images/21521/Deflected%20MA.png)



Basically this is the Moving Average Envelopes (MAE).
But unlike the original, has a greater choice of methods for calculating moving averages.
Top, Bottom, Central line may have different methods of calculation.
Shift option is also added.
