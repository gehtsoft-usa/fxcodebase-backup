## TrendLine_Cross_Trading

**Apprentice** · Mon Aug 25, 2025 5:24 am

![EURUSD m1 (08-25-2025 1215).png](images/160358/EURUSD%20m1%20%2808-25-2025%201215%29.png)



Opens a trade when the price crosses the user-drawn line. The line is manually defined by the user.

 

![Bez naslova.png](images/160358/Bez%20naslova.png)



If necessary You must initiate the line on the main chart and then add it to the oscillator.
If you try to initiate it directly on the oscillator, the option won’t be available.
