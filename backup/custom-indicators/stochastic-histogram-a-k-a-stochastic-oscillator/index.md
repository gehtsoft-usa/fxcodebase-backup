Stochastic Histogram a.k.a. Stochastic Oscillator Is basically an ordinary Stochastic minus 50
Stochastic Oscillator = Stochastic -50;
For this reason, oscillate around the zero line.

I have added the option of choosing the zero line mode.
As the zero line, you can now use the moving average of Stochasti.
Stochastic Oscillator = Stochastic -MA of Stochastic;
I have noticed that in this way have an early indication.
