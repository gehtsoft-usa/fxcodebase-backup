## XMA_RLH indicator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Jan 28, 2013 4:41 pm

This indicator is a ported MQL5 indicators from [http://www.mql5.com/en/code/1447](http://www.mql5.com/en/code/1447)

Formula:
XMA_RLH=2*MA1(Price)-MA2(Price).
