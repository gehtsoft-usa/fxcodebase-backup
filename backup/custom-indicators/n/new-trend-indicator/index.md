## New Trend Indicator

**Apprentice** · Fri Sep 16, 2011 2:35 pm

This indicator attempts to detect the emergence of a new trend.

Up Trend
Close> MVA + ATR

Down Trend
Close <MVA - ATR

Unspecified Trend - everything in between.
