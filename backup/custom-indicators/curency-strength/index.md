## Curency Strength

**Apprentice** · Sun Nov 20, 2011 3:35 pm

![Curency Pair Strength.png](images/18259/Curency%20Pair%20Strength.png)



This indicator generates a list of all the currency pairs that contain a defined currency.
The indicator has a Sorting function.
Enable you to generation inverse pairs in order to have selected Currency on second place in pair.

 [Curency Strength.lua](files/18259/Curency%20Strength.lua)
