## Gopalakrishnan_Range_Index

**Apprentice** · Sat Jan 18, 2020 5:55 am

![GBPUSD D1 (01-18-2020 1002).png](images/130782/GBPUSD%20D1%20%2801-18-2020%201002%29.png)



Gopalakrishnan indicator or GAPO for short is a measurement of market volatility.
The indicator is calculated over a set time period (default 5 days) using only price action.
Developed by Jayanthi Gopalakrishnan which is based on Mandelbrot's work, to measure the dynamic character of the financial markets.
