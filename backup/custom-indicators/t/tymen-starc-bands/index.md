Formulas:
Middle=MA,
Upper=MA+KATR*ATR,
Lower=MA-KATR*ATR,
MUpper=MA+MKATR*ATR,
MLower=MA-MKATR*ATR, where
KATR, MKATR is a indicator parameters.
