## TTI RSIV-1 indicator

**Apprentice** · Thu Sep 29, 2022 1:04 am

![EURUSD m1 (09-29-2022 0803).png](images/147648/EURUSD%20m1%20%2809-29-2022%200803%29.png)



Based on the code
[https://www.prorealcode.com/prorealtime ... indicator/](https://www.prorealcode.com/prorealtime-indicators/tti-rsiv-1-indicator/)

The RSIV-1 is able to adapt quickly to the change in trading direction and thus give back-to-back long and short signals. The primary reason that RSIV-1 can adapt quickly is due to the nature of the linear regression it uses. Linear regression can adapt to direction changes relatively fast.

Description from the author of the indicator: Kirk Northington
