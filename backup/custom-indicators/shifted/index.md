## Shifted

**Apprentice** · Thu Nov 28, 2013 5:27 am

![Shifted.png](images/91187/Shifted.png)



This indicator will introduce a modification of standard shift Indicators.

Vertical shift can be defined in pips or as absolutely value.
It can be applied in both, indicator and oscillators alike.
