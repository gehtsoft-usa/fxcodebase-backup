## Re: 3_Level_ZZ_Semafor

**mulligan** · Tue Aug 11, 2015 10:26 am

Stance,
I am a trader like yourself, not an expert programmer as the folks here at Codebase. Back testing a strategy based on an indicator that repaints will not give you real world results. Neither will looking at history as it is a perfect history. It does not reflect how many times the indicator showed up then disappeared. I have used the ZigZag Strategy as an alert with allow strategy to trade set to no. It is simply a heads up to look at the situation and consider many other factors before placing a trade. Using this strategy or any other based on a repainting indicator to actually make trades is, in my opinion, a very bad idea. If you use the strategy as an alert, you will see that indeed it matches the indicator. An indicator that comes and goes comes and goes (a lot).
