If "Add Body" is on and Add Wick is off.
The indicator will be the sum of up and down Candle Body in last N periods.

My initial assumption was.
Down wicks give bullish signal.
Up wicks give bearish signal.

Observation did not confirm this assumption.

I noticed wicks are contrarian signal,
therefore inverse logic has been used.

If used wicks are added fo sum.
