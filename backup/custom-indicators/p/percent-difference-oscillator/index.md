## Percent Difference Oscillator

**Apprentice** · Mon Mar 18, 2013 1:31 pm

![Percent Difference Oscillator.png](images/56822/Percent%20Difference%20Oscillator.png)



As described by Darryl W. Maddox in the November 1990 issue of S & C magazine.
Percent Difference Oscillator will show difference between the current day's
close and the moving average, expressing the difference as a percentage of moving average.
Percentage above / below moving average.
PDO = 100*(Close-MA)/MA
