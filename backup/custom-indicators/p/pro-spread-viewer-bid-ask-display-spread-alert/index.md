## PRO Spread Viewer (bid & ask display + spread alert)

**Phil_RX** · Fri Oct 23, 2015 4:48 am

**OVERVIEW :**

**DESCRIPTION :**
PRO SPREAD VIEWER is simple to describe, it will show you realtime bid and ask price of the current displayed instrument. Most of the people forget that trading is subject to spreads between the available price to buy and sell. They often don’t understand why their stops get runned as the candle haven’t reached the stop price. Its because they forgot about this bid/ask spread. This indicator will highlight this difference, and also alert you if the current spread is above a certain trigger level that you’ll specify. With this tool you will be able to enter the market a the best possible spread condition. Avoiding to jump in to quickly when the broket is offering wide spreads. Sometime waiting for a few seconds to let the spread tighten a little bit can be a lot more rewarding !
