## Power weighted moving average (PWMA)

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Sep 05, 2011 11:47 am

PWMA = SUM(Price(i)*(i^Power), Period)/SUM(i^Power, Period).
For Power=1 PWMA is identical to Linear Weighted Moving Average (LWMA).
