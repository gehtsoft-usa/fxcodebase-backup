## PRO Clock (chart clock with advanced feat.)

**Phil_RX** · Fri Oct 23, 2015 4:46 am

**OVERVIEW :**

**DESCRIPTION :**
PRO CLOCK is a very small tool but yet so powerful and highly addictive ! Once you’ll test you’ll simply never get rid of it ! Of course having a clock displayed on your chart seem quite normal. Actually the Trading Station is desperately missing of this crucial tool ! Timing is a real important aspect of trading. Anyway PRO Clock couldn’t be a simple clock display tool ! It had to offer some advanced features to deserve its PRO name ! In this tool you’ll have the ability to add time related notification that will be displayed within you chart canvas. These notification will be triggered depending on a time basis that you’ll specify. When this trigger is reached, a notification text is displayed at the center of the chart for a specified amount of time (10s by default). Don’t want to miss an H4 bar close of the market opening ? Well this indicator is designed to prevent you from missing these precious advantage of anticipation !

You’ll have 5 notification slots, each with a time trigger, and a customisable text to be displayed. Color, text font and notification duration are customisable. Of course the clock display itself is customisable and has transparency features so the price never gets hidden.
