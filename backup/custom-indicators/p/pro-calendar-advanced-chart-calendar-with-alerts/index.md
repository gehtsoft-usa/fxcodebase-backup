## PRO Calendar (advanced chart calendar with alerts)

**Phil_RX** · Sat Feb 13, 2016 7:55 am

**OVERVIEW :**

**DESCRIPTION :**
PRO CALENDAR is of course an economic calendar but obviously the PRO version had to go even further than what’s already available here & there. Indeed this indicator offers an inovative and convenient design in addition to the basic settings available in every similar indicators. The additions concern the way it will be displayed on your chart (this way you’ll have plenty of different ways to display and many different combinations). PRO CALENDAR updates automatically as the time passes and of course offers some alert features. Every trader have already been trapped by an volatilee move someday due to an economic release he hasn’t anticipated… Well that’s exactly why I created this indicator. It will alert you in advance, actually the whole indicator display have been designed to this purpose of anticipating news and never ever get surprised by something you missed !
