## Multi fractals

**refresc0** · Thu May 01, 2014 11:30 am

This is my first indicator and hope you enjoy it!
It shows 5 different type of fractals for both Up and Down so total of 10. (See image for types).
This indicator helps avoid false fractals. It's not a strategy, it simply displays the fractals.

 

![fractal types.JPG](images/93765/fractal%20types.JPG)

 [Multi Fractal with Alert.lua](files/93765/Multi%20Fractal%20with%20Alert.lua)
