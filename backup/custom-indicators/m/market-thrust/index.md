## MARKET THRUST

**Apprentice** · Tue Mar 19, 2013 1:38 pm

![MT.png](images/56909/MT.png)



As described in the article, Market Thrust by Tushar S. Chande.
MT= ((AI*AV)- (DI*DV))/Scaling

AI = # Advancing issues
AV = Advancing volume
DI = # Declining issues
DV = Declining volume
