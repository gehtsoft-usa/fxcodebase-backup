## MACD DMI EMA Overlay

![MACD DMI  EMA Overlay.png](images/26519/MACD%20DMI%20EMA%20Overlay.png)



This indicator Overlay combines MACD, EMA & DMI.
You can use all three of them to filter Price Action,
or any combination ot then.

EMA Filter

Long - EMA >EMA(-1)
Short - EMA <EMA(-1)

DMI Filter

Long - DIP > DIM
Short - DIP < DIM

MACD Filter
MACD > SIGNAL
MACD < SIGNAL
