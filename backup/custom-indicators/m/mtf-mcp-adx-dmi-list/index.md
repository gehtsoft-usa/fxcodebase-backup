GREEN UP ARROW:
 ADX(PERIOD)> ADX(PERIOD-1)
 AND ADX > ENTRY LEVEL
AND ADX < EXIT LEVEL
 AND DMI[POSITIVE]> DMI[NEGATIVE]

RED DOWN ARROW:
 ADX(PERIOD)> ADX(PERIOD-1)
AND ADX > ENTRY LEVEL
 AND ADX < EXIT LEVEL
 AND DMI[POSITIVE]< DMI[NEGATIVE]
