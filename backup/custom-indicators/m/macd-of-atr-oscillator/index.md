## MACD of ATR oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Nov 07, 2011 10:58 am

This indicator is the MACD which, instead of two MA uses 2 ATR.

Formulas:
MACD=ATR(Short period)-ATR(Long period),
Signal=MA(MACD),
Histogram=MACD-Signal.
