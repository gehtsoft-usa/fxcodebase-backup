## MA_St oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Thu Feb 23, 2012 10:19 am

This oscillator is similar to stochastic.

Formulas:
MA_St[i]=100*(Price[i]-MA[i])/Range, where
Range=Max-Min,
Max, Min are a maximum and minimum values (Price-MA) from (i-Period) to (i).
