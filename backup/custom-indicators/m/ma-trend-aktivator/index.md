## MA Trend Aktivator

**Apprentice** · Sun Jul 03, 2022 1:43 am

![EURUSD m1 (03-15-2021 0210).png](images/146589/EURUSD%20m1%20%2803-15-2021%200210%29.png)



MA Trend Aktivator a.k.a. Britains 89WMA Trend Aktivator

This indicator act as a trend following system by itself. Based on an moving average, the trend reversal is triggered by the crosses of the high or low with the Aktivator line.
