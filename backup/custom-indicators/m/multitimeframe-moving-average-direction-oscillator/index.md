## Multitimeframe Moving Average Direction Oscillator

**Nikolay.Gekht** · Fri Apr 23, 2010 12:27 pm

![EURUSD m1 (04-04-2017 1103).png](images/1398/EURUSD%20m1%20%2804-04-2017%201103%29.png)



The oscillator show the direction (up or down) of four moving averages (the method and parameter can be chosen for each moving average individually). Each of these moving averages can be applied at the specified time frame (the time frame must be the same or bigger than the chart time frame).

The direction is shown as green (up) or red (down) square, the first moving average is the highest square, the last - is the lowest square:

Download:

 [mtf_mva_dir.lua](files/1398/mtf_mva_dir.lua)

The indicator was revised and updated
