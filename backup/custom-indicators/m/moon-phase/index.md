Moon oscillates between full and new during a period of 30 days.

There is no magic to this, if you plot it you gonna get a constant sinewave.

Given that the market moves in waves/cycles, of course there will be a time that the peaks and throughs will align with the lunar phases sinewave, this is no different then using a cycle indicator, which is better because you can adjust the cycle when it gets out of sync.
