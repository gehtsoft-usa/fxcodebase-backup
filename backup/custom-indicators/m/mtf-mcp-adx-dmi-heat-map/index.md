## MTF MCP ADX DMI Heat Map

**Apprentice** · Thu Feb 06, 2014 5:27 am

![MTF MCP ADX DMI Heat Map.png](images/92535/MTF%20MCP%20ADX%20DMI%20Heat%20Map.png)



One of the four filters can be applied.
**ADX**
ADX compared to ADX Level

**DMI**
Relationship between positive and negative DMI components.

**ADX + DMI**
Consensus of Two

**ADX+ ADX Slope + DMI**
Consensus of Two
If ADX Is Rising, ADX + DMI indication is confirmed
If ADX is Falling, Neutral indication is given.
