## MTF MCP Stochastic RSI Heat Map

![AUDCAD H3 (09-19-2017 1058).png](images/41087/AUDCAD%20H3%20%2809-19-2017%201058%29.png)



Gives an indication based on the position of components of Stochastic RSI.
K / D Line position.
K> D Up Trend
K <D Down Trend

K / Cental Line position.
K> 50 Up Trend
K <50 Down Trend
