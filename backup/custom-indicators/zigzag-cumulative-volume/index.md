Hi brother apprentice! I started a request very similar to this.

In fact, you can tweak this indi you posted here it will be the same thing I asked.

The extras I need in the ZigZag Cumulative Volume:

-The option to choose it to show the average volume of each leg (instead of simple summing). Which is, the total volume of each leg divided by the number of bars that make up the leg, displayed as numerical value on top of the pivot of an up leg and below the pivot of a down leg.

-You can choose to display the average, the total, or both at the same time (I mean the numerical value on top and below the zigzag pivots)

-Can turn on and off the histogram. Be able to choose if the histogram will be plotted based on the total sum of volume or average volume.

-The ability of Multi timeframe display. For example, load the indi on a 5min chart and choose to display the zigzag of 15min or 30min.
