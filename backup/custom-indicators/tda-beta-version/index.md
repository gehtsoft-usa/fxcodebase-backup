## TDA beta version

**richardtao** · Wed Jan 04, 2012 12:18 am

The TDA indicator is counted as serials number 7.
The purpose is measuring momentum to predict next force.
The beta version is still on testing right now. It’s not perfect yet.
The recommended action would take at next period when signal appears.
Since this is a predictor, the price may or may not reverse after the signal appeared.
The possibility of signal is listed from high to low:
1.The boldface vertical arrows.
2.The boldface diagonal arrows.
3.The vertical simple arrows.
4.The diagonal simple arrows.
5.The hollow turning arrows.
6.The hollow vertical arrows.
7.The hollow diagonal arrows.
The hollow arrows recommend that the action of squaring position rather than opening position.
