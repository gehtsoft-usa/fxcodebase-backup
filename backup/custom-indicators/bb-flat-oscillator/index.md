This indicator is a ported MQL5 indicator from [http://www.mql5.com/ru/code/1729](http://www.mql5.com/ru/code/1729) (in Russian).

Formulas:
MA[i] = Price[i] - Moving average[i],
Upper band, Lower band - range of bollinger bands.
