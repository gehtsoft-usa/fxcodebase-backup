## Averages Awesome Oscillator Convergence divergence

**Apprentice** · Sun Sep 21, 2014 6:16 am

![Averages Awesome Oscillator Convergence divergence.png](images/96038/Averages%20Awesome%20Oscillator%20Convergence%20divergence.png)



Unlike regular awesome this indicator uses Tick source.
That means u can select any price component.
Regular awesome indicator uses median component.

In addition, the moving average method can be changed.
Additionally the signal line and the histogram lines are added.
The signal line is a moving average of the awesome line.
The histogram is the difference between these two, very similar to the MACD.

One of the three lines can be displayed in bar form.
