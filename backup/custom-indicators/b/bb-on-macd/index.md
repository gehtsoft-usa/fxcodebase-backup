BB lines can be calculated based on any of the MACD lines
BB lines can be placed on any of the MACD lines or as calculated
A)
Top = MACD Placement + BB Deviation of Source
Bottom = MACD Placement - Deviation of Source
B)
Top = BB Top Line of Source
Bottom = BB Bottom Line of Source
