## Reverse Engineered RSI

**Apprentice** · Wed Jan 02, 2013 2:17 pm

![Reverse Engineered RSI.png](images/49362/Reverse%20Engineered%20RSI.png)



 Reverse Engineered RSI transforms a horizontal lines, 30, 50, 70 from the relative strength index (RSI) graph to the price graph.

 [Reverse Engineered RSI.lua](files/49362/Reverse%20Engineered%20RSI.lua)

 [Reverse Engineered RSI with Alert.lua](files/49362/Reverse%20Engineered%20RSI%20with%20Alert.lua)

This indicator will provides Audio / Email Alerts if and when Price cross OB/OS or Central Line.
