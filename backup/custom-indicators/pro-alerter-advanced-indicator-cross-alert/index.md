## PRO Alerter (advanced indicator cross alert)

**Phil_RX** · Fri Oct 23, 2015 4:32 am

**OVERVIEW :**

**DESCRIPTION :**
PRO ALERTER is the utlimate tool that allows you to turn any indicator into a PRO indicator ! You already know that my goal is to provide alerts in nearly all the indicators available, well, then this indicators is allowing you to trigger cross alert with litterally every indicator ! Want to be alerted of a moving average cross ? A bollinger band cross ? You’re at the exact right place ! Load it in the PRO ALERTER and it will do the job for you ! You can of course choose to hide entirely the indicator output but still trigger the alerts. Showing you only what needs to be seen. I’ve also added the ability to color the output differently if they are increasing ou decreasing. This feature is really perfect for moving averages ! They can be drawn blue when going up and red when going down for exemple ! Much more visually efficient to read. Of course this indicators is suitable for mutli timeframe usage ! This tool is really a must have and its absolutely 100% free so don »t hesitate to hit the download button !
