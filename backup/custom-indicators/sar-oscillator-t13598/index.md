## SAR Oscillator

**Apprentice** · Sat Feb 18, 2012 9:55 am

![SAR Oscillator.png](images/26298/SAR%20Oscillator.png)



SARO = Avg ( close -SAR );

If MA period is 1,
we get the actual distance between the SAR and closing price.

One of the purposes, to detect the strength of the trend.

The strengthening trend
Prices move away from the Sar

The weakening trend
Price is approaching toward Sar
