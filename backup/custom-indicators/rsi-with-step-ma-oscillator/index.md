## RSI with step MA oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Fri Nov 29, 2013 2:01 pm

Formulas:
RSI[i] = 100-(100/(1+Positive/Negative)), where
Positive, Negative - sum of positive and negative changes of Diff,
Diff[i] = MA[i]-MA[i-Step].
