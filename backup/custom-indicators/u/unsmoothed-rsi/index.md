**Apprentice** · Mon Jun 24, 2013 5:05 am

![Unsmoothed RSI.png](images/68218/Unsmoothed%20RSI.png)



RSI formula is (100 – (100 / (1 + RS)))
where RS = 14 day EMA of gains/losses.

In this version we use Unsmoothed data.
