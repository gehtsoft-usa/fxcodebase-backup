## PRO Manager (shortcut trade with your keyboard)

**Phil_RX** · Fri Oct 23, 2015 5:09 am

**OVERVIEW :**

**DESCRIPTION :**
PRO MANAGER will transform your Trading Station into one of the most performant professionnal trading software! You’ll be able to manage your trades with a single move using totally exclusive keyboard mapping shortcuts. Everything is entirely customisable to make your own configuration. Step by Step tutorial explained in the demonstration video. Pro manager entirely changes the way you will trade within the Trading Station !
Automatic Stop & Limit with different presets, close & entry shortcuts, etc. There is also a total rework of the order display. The default orders bars of the TS are no longer necessary, the indicator offers the ability to replace this with a much discrete display (you can of course keep using the classic display). There is also a built-in Watermark switch that will automatically hide your opened trades and any other trading information.
There is also a built-in Watermark switch that will automatically hide your opened trades and any other trading information. So that you can share pure trading analysis within your favorite social networks. This indicator may seem very complicated in the first place but believe me once you’ll try it and get used to it you’ll never let it go !
