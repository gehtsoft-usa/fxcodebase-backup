## Day fibo lines

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Tue Dec 18, 2012 3:04 pm

The indicator draws the daily range lines and fibo lines between them.

 

![Day_Fibo_Lines.PNG](images/48728/Day_Fibo_Lines.PNG)



Fibo levels:
L - 0 %
F1 - 23.6 %
F2 - 38.2 %
F3 - 50 %
F4 - 61.8 %
F5 - 76.4 %
H - 100 %

Download:

 [Day_Fibo_Lines.lua](files/48728/Day_Fibo_Lines.lua)

 [Day_Fibo_Lines with Alert.lua](files/48728/Day_Fibo_Lines%20with%20Alert.lua)

 [Week_Fibo_Lines.lua](files/48728/Week_Fibo_Lines.lua)

Day_Fibo_Lines with Alert give Audio / Email Alerts if Price cross over/under or touch one of fib. levels.
