## Donchian Oscillator

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Mon Nov 01, 2010 11:39 pm

If Close>max of previous [Period] Bars Oscillator=1
If Close<min of previous [Period] Bars Oscillator=-1

For current bar close price equivalent bid or ask.
