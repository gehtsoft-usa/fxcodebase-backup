## Displaced Moving Average

**Apprentice** · Mon Dec 05, 2022 8:24 am

![ETHUSD H1 (12-05-2022 1421).png](images/148572/ETHUSD%20H1%20%2812-05-2022%201421%29.png)



The DMA (Displaced Moving Averages) indicator places three Moving Averages
6-period Moving Average displaced forward on 3 periods
12-period Moving Average displaced forward on 6 periods
24-period Moving Average displaced forward on 12 periods
The indicator is drawn on the basis of the description given in Joe DiNapoli's book "Trading with DiNapoli Levels".
