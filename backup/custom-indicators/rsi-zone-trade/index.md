![RSI Zone Trade.png](images/5014/RSI%20Zone%20Trade.png)



Positive Zone (green)
RSI (x) > 50
AND
RSI(x) > Moving Average(X) of RSI

Negative Zone (Red)
RSI (x) < 50
AND
RSI(x) < Moving Average(X) of RSI
