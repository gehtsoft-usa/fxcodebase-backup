## Re: TMACD Divergence

**Apprentice** · Wed Nov 03, 2010 5:46 am

Divergences signal a potential trend reversal or can be used as a possible sign for a trend continuation.

In this case, detects, break point of the current trend.
Meaning trend change is possible, not certain.

Because of the way, that the divergence it reveals itself, an indicator must lag in the calculation.
But when trading divergence usually talk about the movement of prices in the future.
