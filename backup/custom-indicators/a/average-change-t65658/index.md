![USDSEK m1 (01-22-2018 1021).png](images/117181/USDSEK%20m1%20%2801-22-2018%201021%29.png)



Average Change calculated as the difference between open and close,
high and low.
The moving average is then applied to this data.

 [Average Change.lua](files/117181/Average%20Change.lua)

 [Average Change List.lua](files/117181/Average%20Change%20List.lua)

Additionally, Average Change List will calculate/create a list of average one minute change for last 10 hours.
Index 1 will represent Last hour.
Average Change List can only be applied to a one-minute time frame.
