## Arms Index (TRIN)

**Apprentice** · Sat Jan 19, 2019 7:27 am

![EURUSD H1 (11-01-2018 0911).png](images/123442/EURUSD%20H1%20%2811-01-2018%200911%29.png)



Short-Term TRading INdex, the Arms Index is a breadth indicator developed by Richard W. Arms in 1967. The index is calculated by dividing the Advance-Decline Ratio by the Advance-Decline Volume Ratio.
Arms Index = (advances / declines) / (advancing_volume / declining_volume);
 Typically derived for stocks in this example was modified for forex.
