Based on request.
[viewtopic.php?f=27&t=62655](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=27&t=62655)
if current bar is higher than the previous one then colour is green,
if current bar is lower than the one previous then colour its red.
if N consecutive tick volume bars are higher then bar is dark green,
if N consecutive tick volume bars are lower then bar is dark red.
