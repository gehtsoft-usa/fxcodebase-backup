## XMA Range Bands

**Alexander.Gettinger** · Fri Jan 18, 2013 5:50 pm

This indicator is a ported MQL5 indicators from [viewtopic.php?f=27&t=27919](https://fxcodebase.com/code/viewtopic.php?f=27&t=27919)

Formulas:
XMA=MA(Price),
Upper=XMA+MA(Range),
Lower=XMA-MA(Range), where
Range=High-Low.
