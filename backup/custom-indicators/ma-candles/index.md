The rules for the colour change are;

1) if the close of the price bar is above the SMA, the bar is GREEN
2) if the close of the price bar is below the SMA, the bar is RED
3) if either of these conditions are not met, the bar is GREY

If High/Low Filter is used.
The rules for the colour change are;
1) if the close, and the low of the price bar is above the SMA, the bar is GREEN
2) if the close and the high of the price bar is below the SMA, the bar is RED
3) if either of these conditions are not met, the bar is GREY
